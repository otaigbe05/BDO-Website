**The short answer:** for a small, appointment-based business in Ontario, a website usually costs one of three ways. Building it yourself on a website builder runs about **$15 to $60 a month**. A custom site from a designer or agency is about **$2,000 to $10,000** up front, plus hosting and updates. A monthly plan that includes the build and the upkeep is about **$50 to $200 a month**. Those ranges come from a November 2026 Canadian price guide; add a domain name, email, and 13 per cent HST on top.

The right choice depends less on the price tag than on one question: who will keep the site up to date, and how much is your time worth?

## The three ways to get a website

| Option | Up front | Every month | Best for | What it costs you in time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Do it yourself on a builder | $0 | About $15 to $60 for the plan | Owners who like to tinker and have evenings free | The most: building, writing, photos and every update |
| A designer or agency builds it | About $2,000 to $10,000 | Hosting and updates, often $30 to $150 if someone else does them | Businesses that want a custom look and have a budget | Meetings and approvals; updates go back to the designer |
| A monthly plan (build plus care) | $0 to about $1,500 | About $50 to $200 | Owners who want it done and kept current, without technical work | The least: you send changes, someone else makes them |

Ranges: WebDesignGuide's 2026 Canadian price guide (a site that publishes web design guides, so treat them as a guide, not a quote).

Builder plans are often priced in US dollars. Wix, for example, lists plans from US$17 a month paid annually for its basic plan to US$39 a month for its Business plan, according to Expert Market's 2026 review. Check the builder's own pricing page with Canada selected before you decide, because prices and plan names change.

## The costs people forget

The build is only part of the bill. These are the lines that surprise people, with typical ranges from the same guide:

- **Domain name** (yourshop.ca): about $15 to $30 a year. Register it in your own name.
- **Email at your domain** (you@yourshop.ca): about $8 to $15 per person per month for Google Workspace or similar. Optional, but it looks more professional than a free address.
- **Hosting:** $5 to $60 a month if it is not included in your plan.
- **Updates and security:** $30 to $150 a month if someone else does them.
- **Photos:** $300 to $1,500 for a photographer. Good phone photos of your own work, in good light, are often enough for a small shop.
- **Writing:** $100 to $400 a page if you pay someone to write it.
- **Online booking:** some booking apps charge a monthly fee, and some take a cut of each booking or charge per staff member. Read the pricing page all the way down.
- **HST:** 13 per cent in Ontario, usually not included in the prices you see.

## What you are actually paying for

For a barbershop, a tattoo studio, an auto shop, a salon or a trainer, a website has a short list of jobs. If it does these well, it is worth the money; if it does not, a cheaper or more expensive site will not fix it.

1. **Prices and services**, written so a customer can decide without calling.
2. **Hours, address and phone**, correct and easy to tap on a phone.
3. **Photos of your real work**, your space and your team.
4. **A Book button** that goes straight to a page where they pick a time.
5. **The basics for Google:** a clear page title and description, your city in the text, and a link from your Google Business Profile. Google says local results depend on relevance, distance and popularity, and a complete, accurate profile and website help with the first one.

Everything else, such as animations, blogs and fancy layouts, is optional.

## How to compare two quotes

Put these questions to anyone who quotes you, including us:

- **Who owns the domain and the site?** You should own the domain. Ask what happens to your site if you stop paying.
- **What does the monthly fee include?** Hosting, security updates, content changes (how many a month?), and support.
- **How do changes work?** Can you change prices and hours yourself, or do you send an email and wait?
- **Is booking included, or extra?** If it is extra, what does it cost per month, per staff member, or per booking?
- **Does the price include HST?**
- **Can you see sites they built for businesses like yours?** Look at them on your phone, not just on their laptop.

A low price with a long contract, a domain you do not own, or changes you cannot make can cost more in the end than a higher price without them.
